# Welcome to pgdemo

Short, hands-on lessons on modern PostgreSQL 19. Each one runs a few real commands, shows you what changed, and ends with a small challenge you solve yourself.

## The screen

**Lesson pane** (left, where you are now). It explains each step and runs it when you press **Enter**. The keys you can use are always listed at the bottom.

**SQL pane** (top right). Your own Postgres prompt, already connected to the lesson. Click it to type a query, then click back here to carry on.

**Live View** (bottom right). The numbers the lesson wants you to watch. It refreshes every second, so you can see what each step changes.

**Tabs** (along the bottom). Extra tools you can click any time. **Top** shows what the server is doing right now. Click **Lesson** to come back.

## Break things

Each lesson has its own database, called its playground. Try anything you like in it. If it gets into a mess, `pgdemo 1 reset` rebuilds it.

Press **Enter** to begin.
